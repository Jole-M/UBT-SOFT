FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is UBT?

UBT is an AI-powered automation layer designed to help creators and businesses generate and publish short-form video content at scale.

Which platforms can I use?

The workflow is designed around short-form platforms such as Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Exact integrations depend on your UBT setup.

Is UBT really $50 per month?

Yes. The landing-page offer is presented as one plan at $50 per month. Your checkout or payment link can be connected to the Start Now buttons.

Do I need technical skills?

No coding is required for the intended workflow, although your account and publishing setup may require initial configuration.
